  kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "obsidian.fullname" . }} 3000:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  Open http://127.0.0.1:3000 (a reverse proxy must serve it over HTTPS for
  normal use: the stream needs a secure browser context and WebSocket upgrades).

who reaches it gets a shell in the container.

  In-cluster URL: http://{{ include "obsidian.couchdb.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc.cluster.local:{{ .Values.couchdb.service.port }}
  - A proxy in front of it must pass CORS preflights through; CouchDB answers
    them for app://obsidian.md and capacitor://localhost.
  - Never expose /_utils (Fauxton) to the internet.
  - Turn on LiveSync end-to-end encryption with a passphrase.
